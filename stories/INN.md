# The inn at the end of yesterday

Three guests arrive after midnight. Each signs the register with the thing
they cannot put down.

```text
bottle the guest who arrived tomorrow
bottle the room with no window
bottle a suitcase full of rain
tide
```

There is one spare room. If you want it, type `bottle` followed by what you
are carrying. Press Enter on an empty line when everyone is ready to sleep.

```text
dream
```

The inn has thin walls. In the night, the guests hear one another dreaming.
Find a line that sounds as though it belongs to two people at once.

```text
strandline
postcard
```

At breakfast, nobody agrees on the names in the register. Open the postcard
and choose which name you will answer to. You may `sign` that name in the
guestbook before you leave.

```text
quit
```

You leave the spare key with the sea.
