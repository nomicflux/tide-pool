# After the gallery closes

After the visitors leave, the frames start speaking. Someone has left you
the keys.

```text
visit
```

Type `tour` followed by a word to find the halls that remember it, or
choose from the list. Enter with `visit` followed by the hall’s name; its
last line will come with you. If there are no halls yet, you are here
before the first exhibition.

Continue with an empty Enter.

```text
drop frame dust key
tide
```

Read the poem as something said by a frame after its picture has gone.
You may type `bottle` followed by your answer before continuing.

```text
tide
postcard
```

Open `postcard.svg` through Keepsakes or the printed path. Decide where you
would hang it.

Leave the keys where the morning attendant will find them.
