# The lighthouse keeper's last shift

You have been keeping a light for a ship that never arrives. Tonight, leave
three things in the water:

```text
drop lighthouse coat letter
tide
```

Decide who wrote the letter.

The beam disappears before the tower does.

```text
weather fog
drop ship window
tide
```

Decide what the fog is keeping to itself.

```text
weather storm
tide
```

Keep one line from this storm in your head. You cannot rescue everything.

At dawn, something washes back.

```text
comb
weather calm
tide
shells
```

The shell may come from tonight or from a stranger's earlier walk along this
same shore. Find a line that could be an answer to the letter.

```text
quit
```

The light goes out. The shore remains.
