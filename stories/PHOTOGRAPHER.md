# The photographer of departures

The photographer has set her camera facing away from the sea.
She says the light is better where someone has just left.

```text
bottle the chair where you almost sat
bottle the hand just outside the frame
```

There is space for another absence. If you wish, `bottle` what is missing
from this picture, then continue.

```text
tide
postcard
```

Open `postcard.svg`. The photographer asks you to hold still.
It is the empty space beside you she is trying to catch.
