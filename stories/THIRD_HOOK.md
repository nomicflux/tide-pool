# The third hook

Two coats are drying by the inn door. Between them, one brass hook is
empty, as if someone had kept it for you.

```text
horizon
find
```

If a bottle came in, read it slowly: someone left room before they knew
your name. If none came, the hook was kept all the same.

```text
bottle a coat that has walked a long way
bottle a hook still warm from waiting
tide
```

Read the poem as the label sewn inside your collar.

At this pause, you may `sign` the guestbook with the name you came under.
Continue with an empty Enter.

```text
guestbook
```

Your name hangs beside theirs now, or the page keeps a line for it.

```text
signal three coats
```

Watch `signal.svg` or play `signal.wav`. From the harbour, the lamp counts
the coats by the door.
