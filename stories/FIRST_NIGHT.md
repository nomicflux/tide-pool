# First night: a score for six bottles

Put six things on the shore. None of them will fit in your pocket.

```text
bottle the room we left behind
bottle the light across the water
bottle a name under the tongue
bottle the key that fits no door
bottle the bell below the harbour
bottle the last train home
weather calm
tide
```

Read the poem as a set of invitations.

```text
weather storm
tide
```

Read the poem as messages left on a locked door.

```text
weather fog
tide
postcard
listen
bind
```

In fog, read the poem as the beginning of a letter you cannot finish.

Keep the drawing, the bells, or the book. In the browser, use the player and
Keepsakes links. In the terminal, open the printed file paths. The book
includes any poems already left on this shore.

```text
quit
```

Morning will find all six bottles where you left them.
