# No address on the glass

You wait on the harbour steps for a bottle with no address.

```text
horizon
```

Count the bottles without opening them. A bottle you cast during this visit
stays beyond your reach, though a later visit may bring it back.

```text
find
```

If a message arrived, read it as though it has travelled a long way. It may
be your own handwriting returning.

```text
drop cork doorstep
tide
```

At this pause, you may type `cast` followed by a message for a later visitor.
The bottle waits at sea until someone finds it, even after you leave.
Continue with an empty Enter.

```text
postcard
```

Open `postcard.svg` through Keepsakes or the printed path. Keep a picture of
what the tide brought you.

The next knock is for whoever comes after.
