# A frequency for the missing

The station closed years ago. Tonight, someone has left the receiver warm.

```text
moon
drop antenna moth window
weather fog
tide
listen
```

Open the printed `tide.wav` path in your audio player. These are the station's
call signs. Let them finish before you continue.

The receiver has no dial. You tune it by taking things away.

```text
ebb
ebb
weather storm
tide
listen
```

Reopen `tide.wav`: the recording has changed. Something is trying to speak
quickly, before you lose the signal. Read the poem as its transmission.

You send no reply. You turn the aerial toward the shore.

```text
comb
weather calm
tide
listen
```

Reopen the recording once more. An earlier voice now shares the frequency.
Choose one word from the final poem as the name of the person at the other end.

```text
quit
```

Leave the receiver warm.
