# A market for tomorrow

The market opens before anyone has agreed what morning looks like.
A keeper asks the shore for yesterday's answer.

```text
ask what does morning look like
```

If a line answers, hang it above the first stall. If the shore is quiet,
leave the sign blank for now.

```text
bottle a silver fish wrapped in yesterday
bottle a loaf warm enough to remember you
tide
```

The sellers read the poem as a price neither can quite afford.

At this pause, you may `bottle` something you would bring to the market.
Continue with an empty Enter.

```text
postcard
```

Open `postcard.svg` through Keepsakes or the printed path.

Someone lifts the shutters. For a moment, every window is a door.
