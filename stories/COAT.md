# The coat that arrived alone

The innkeeper hangs a wet coat beside the stove. Nobody remembers opening
the door. Its pockets are warm.

```text
bottle a coat still warm from another shore
bottle a pocket full of doors
tide
```

Read the poem as the label sewn inside the collar.

```text
tour door
```

If the gallery remembers a door, you may `visit` one of the named halls.
Its last line scatters into the pocket, word by word. If none answers,
the coat has travelled farther than the pictures.
