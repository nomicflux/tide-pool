# A ticket for yesterday

The station has no tracks. Behind the glass, a clerk sells tickets to
hours that have already passed. You ask when the next one leaves.

```text
tidetable
```

Open `tideclock.svg`. The clerk follows the needle with one finger.
“We can promise a departure. We cannot promise the same arrival.”

```text
bottle a ticket to the hour before goodbye
bottle a platform waiting for its train
tide
```

Read the poem as the destination printed on your ticket.

```text
ask who is waiting
```

The shore answers from the other side of the glass.

At this pause, you may `cast` a message to someone you would travel back
for. Continue with an empty Enter.

```text
signal still here
```

Watch `signal.svg` or play `signal.wav`. The lamp spells what the clerk
writes on the back of every ticket.

Outside, the sea keeps arriving.
