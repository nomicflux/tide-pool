# The end of the jetty

Someone has asked you to meet them where the planks stop. Walk out first.

```text
spell keepers
```

Open `jetty.svg` and play `jetty.wav`. Each plank was laid by a tide;
where a letter stands alone, the footsteps fall silent.

```text
horizon
```

Out here the sea is close enough to count. If a bottle is drifting, it may
be the one that asked you to come.

```text
find
```

If a message arrived, read it as the voice of whoever waits at the end. If
nothing came, they are late, or they are you.

```text
rhyme water
```

Lines that end the way "water" ends are the jetty's echo. If none answer,
the boards are too new to echo yet.

At this pause, you may `bottle` something to say when you reach the end.
The jetty adds a footstep and a lantern. Continue with an empty Enter.

```text
drop footstep lantern
tide
```

The last plank creaks with whatever the tide just made. At this pause, you
can `cast` a message back toward the shore for the next person to walk out.

```text
signal here
```

Watch `signal.svg` through Keepsakes or the printed path: from the end of
the jetty, the lamp is only a few planks away.
