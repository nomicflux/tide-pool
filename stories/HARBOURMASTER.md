# An entry in the ledger

The harbourmaster has a column for everything that stays and everything
that leaves. You ask which column holds someone who is expected.

They turn the ledger toward you.

```text
harbour
```

There are the things the coast can count. The harbourmaster taps the blank
margin. “And here are the things we keep a chair for.”

```text
bottle a chair for someone who has not arrived
bottle a name pencilled in the margin
tide
```

Read the poem as an entry the harbourmaster will have to accept.

At this pause, you may `cast` a message for whoever you are waiting for.
Continue with an empty Enter.

```text
harbour
```

The ledger has changed. The chair is still empty. The harbourmaster puts
the kettle on anyway.
