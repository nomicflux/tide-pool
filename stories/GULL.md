# The gull that learned your voice

The gull on the seawall is waiting for you to look away.

```text
bottle a silver button from a borrowed coat
bottle the last word of a letter
bottle a crumb saved for the journey
```

Hold still. It only wants one thing.

```text
ebb
```

Look at what you still have.

```text
tide
```

Read the poem around the missing thing. If you remember its words, you can
`cast` them after the gull for another visitor to find.

Beyond the wall, something is practising your voice.
