# The kitchen at dawn

Below the lamp room is a kitchen. Someone has laid two places, although the
keepers never seem to arrive together.

```text
bottle a cup gone cold
bottle a chair pulled out
bottle the key between us
weather calm
tide
```

Read the poem as the table you found. Decide which place was meant for you.

At this pause, you may type `bottle` followed by something you would leave
for the other keeper. Continue with an empty Enter.

```text
echo
tide
postcard
```

Open `postcard.svg` through Keepsakes or the printed path. Find something
you could carry upstairs without waking anyone.

```text
signal good morning
```

Watch `signal.svg` or hear `signal.wav`. For once, the lamp has no warning
to send.

The kettle clicks off. Upstairs, another hand reaches for the door.
