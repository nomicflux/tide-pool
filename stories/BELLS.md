# The bell-diver's low tide

The village bell fell into the harbour a hundred years ago. Nobody has heard
it since. You are the last diver who knows where it lies. Tonight the tide is
the lowest it will ever be. Bring down what the village gave you:

```text
drop bell rope salt name prayer
look
```

Five gifts, and whatever you were already carrying. The harbour will not let
you bring all of it back up.

```text
tide
ebb
```

Something slipped out of your hands in the dark water. You do not get to
choose what.

```text
weather fog
tide
ebb
```

The fog comes down to the waterline. Up on the quay, someone calls a name.
Was it yours?

```text
weather storm
drop surface breath
tide
```

You rise. The storm takes what it wants on the way up.

```text
ebb
ebb
look
```

On the quay, the village waits for a sound. Instead the sea hands back a line
it has said before:

```text
comb
weather calm
tide
```

Read it out loud. That is the bell, ringing for the first time in a hundred
years.

```text
quit
```

The harbour fills again. Somewhere under it, a rope is still swaying.
