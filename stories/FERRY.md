# The opposite shore

The ferryman asks where you are going. You point across the water.
He looks behind him, as though someone there has called his name.

```text
bottle a shore that can be seen from here
bottle a shore that answers without showing itself
tide
```

The fare is the last thing the sea said. The ferryman holds out his hand.

```text
echo
```

The line comes apart into words as you step aboard.

```text
tide
```

Read the new poem as the bank where you land.
Across the water, someone is pointing toward you.
