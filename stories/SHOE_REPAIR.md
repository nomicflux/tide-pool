# Journeys repaired while you wait

The shoemaker turns your shoes upside down. There is nothing wrong with
them. It is the road that has worn through.

```text
bottle these shoes remember a road that is gone
bottle these shoes have never been home
tide
trace shoes
```

One journey can be repaired; the other will have to be walked.

```text
signal home
```

Open `signal.svg`. If home is not where you are going, type `signal` followed
by your destination. Leave the lamp burning while the shoemaker works.

You step outside barefoot. Behind you, someone begins stitching the road.
