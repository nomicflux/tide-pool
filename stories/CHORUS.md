# A chorus for whoever came before

The shore is rehearsing. Every word left here has a bell; every line has a
place to breathe. Begin with the last voice it remembers.

```text
echo
drop breath bell
tide
recite
```

Open `recital.wav` from the browser's Keepsakes links or the printed path.
The chorus grows with the shore.
Listen for as long as you want before continuing.

Choose a word from the poem. You may type `trace` or `kin` followed by that
word to meet its earlier company. Return with an empty Enter.

```text
bottle the pause between two voices
tide
recite
```

Open the recital again. The newest poem has joined the older ones.

```text
quit
```

Leave a little silence for whoever comes next.
