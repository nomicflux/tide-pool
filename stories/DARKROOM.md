# The darkroom

The photographer slides a sheet of paper into a shallow tray.
For a while, it is only water holding something white.

```text
weather fog
bottle a face the paper almost remembers
bottle a name written on the back in pencil
tide
postcard
```

Open `postcard.svg`. Read what has begun to appear before you continue.
Neither of you breathes on it.

```text
weather calm
tide
postcard
```

Open the postcard again. The water has cleared, and the words have met
one another differently.

The photographer pins the print to a line. You take the blank envelope.
