# The observatory looks down

Above the lighthouse is a room with a telescope pointed at the shore.
The astronomer has been listening instead of looking.

```text
moon
drop lens dust
tide
stars
```

Open `sky.svg`, using the browser's Keepsakes link or the printed file path.
These stars are the words this shore has said most often. The dotted lines
lead to their names; the numbers count their appearances in poems.

The moon follows the date. At new moon, look for its empty patch of sky.

Find a name you did not expect to see in the sky. If a name is missing,
you may leave it with `bottle` followed by the name, then press Enter on
an empty line to continue.

```text
tide
stars
```

Open the sky again. Some names need more than one visit before they appear.

```text
quit
```

On the stairs down, you hear the telescope turning toward someone else.
