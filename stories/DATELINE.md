# The dateline

The shore keeps a ledger of days. Turn back its pages before you add one.

```text
diary
```

Each bar is a day, and each `o` is a poem the tide left. The last line
adds them all up. To read a day’s poems, type `diary` followed by its date.

```text
drop ledger midnight
tide
```

Your poem goes in the ledger under today's date, by the clock at sea.

```text
diary
```

A new bar marks the first poem kept for today. A longer bar means your
poem joins others already here. Either way, the total has gone up by one.
