# The slipway

The ferryman's timetable is pinned to the slipway, curling at the corners.

```text
tidetable
```

Open `tideclock.svg`. If the needle points at low water, the oldest shell
on the coast is within reach of the bottom step.

```text
wade
drop shell slipway
tide
```

If the water was low, you came back with the oldest line in your pocket.
If not, the step stayed under the sea, and you have only what you carried down.

```text
rhyme shell
```

Lines that end like "shell" ring back along the stones, if any do.

```text
crab
```

The crab has watched the whole walk from its borrowed house. Open
`crab.svg` to see which remembered word it chose to live in.
