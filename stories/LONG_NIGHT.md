# The long night

One night both keepers were away at once. Nobody climbed the stairs, nobody
wrote in the log, and the lamp burned on by itself until morning.

You are the first one back. Whatever the sea said last is still in the air.

```text
diary
echo
```

That was the last thing anyone left before the long night. On a new shore,
the log is still blank.

```text
signal still here
```

Watch the lamp in `signal.svg` or hear it in `signal.wav`, through the
browser's Keepsakes links or the printed paths.

You may replace the usual message by typing `signal` followed by your own.
Open the signal again, then continue with an empty Enter.

```text
drop stairs log morning
tide
```

Write the new poem in the log, under the gap where the long night was.

```text
quit
```

The other keeper will see the entry and know the light was never left alone
for long.
