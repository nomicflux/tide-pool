# What the water leaves

At low water, the harbour becomes a room without a roof. Everything floating
here will leave with the tide. Its poems will stay on the shore.

```text
bottle the boat we meant to build
bottle the name we never called
tide
postcard
```

Open the postcard. Take one last look at what the water is holding, including
anything you brought from another walk. Continue when you are ready to let
all of it go.

```text
ebb all
postcard
stars
```

Open the new postcard, then the sky. One is empty; the other remembers.
Find a word that outlasted the thing it named.

Try `wade` for the shore’s oldest shell. If the water is too high, leave it
for another visit. Or `bottle` something you want to begin again with.

The harbour has room for a boat now.
