# The winter the harbour froze

Once in a lifetime the harbour freezes hard enough to walk on. The whole
village goes out onto it, carrying whatever they could not put down all
summer.

```text
weather ice
drop skates breath lamp
bottle a voice under the ice
tide
```

Read the poem slowly. Ice keeps everything exactly where it was put.

```text
listen
postcard
```

Play the recording. The long bells are the ice holding its note. If two
things on the ice should never be parted, type `seek` and their names before
you press Enter. The ice may keep them together.

Open `postcard.svg`. Even the shoreline is holding its breath.

```text
kin breath
```

These are the things your breath has frozen beside, on this shore or on
someone else's walk across it.

The thaw comes all at once, in the night.

```text
weather calm
tide
postcard
```

Read what the open water made of the same things. Nothing was lost; it was
only allowed to move again.

Refresh the postcard. The words rise and settle; the shoreline breathes.

```text
quit
```

In spring, a diver finds a lamp standing upright on the harbour floor.
