# The weather wash

The laundromat takes rain instead of coins. Someone has left a sky in the
last machine, with a note: please wash separately.

```text
drop cloth rain borrowed sky
weather fog
tide
listen
```

Play `tide.wav`. The machine has filled with fog; its window shows only
what you almost remember. Listen before starting the next wash.

```text
weather storm
tide
listen
```

Play the new `tide.wav`. Someone's weather is knocking to be let out.
Read the poem as the note it would leave on your door.

```text
weather calm
tide
listen
postcard
```

Play the final `tide.wav` and open `postcard.svg`. The words have survived
all three washes. Decide which ones are yours.

You hang the sky outside. The clothespins look like small birds waiting
for the wind.
