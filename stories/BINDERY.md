# The bookbinder

The bookbinder works with the window open. Every time a page lifts in the
wind, she holds it down with a stone from the beach.

```text
bottle a page that will not lie flat
bottle a spine still warm from the reader
tide
```

She draws the thread tight.

```text
bind
```

Open `book.html`. Find a line you would fold a corner for.
You may `bottle` that line to carry it away.

Outside, the sea is still turning pages.
