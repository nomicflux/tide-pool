# The night round

The harbourmaster walks the coast once before sleeping and counts what it holds.

```text
harbour
```

The report counts every poem on the shore, and how many were left today.

```text
diary today
```

Today's page. If it is empty, the day is still yours to begin.

```text
drop lantern rope
tide
```

A lantern and a rope join the poem.

```text
harbour
```

One more poem than before, and one more today. You may `sign` your name
before the lamp goes out.
