# Before the first footprint

At the bottom of the steps, a child is trying to remember the shore before
anyone walked on it. You look for the oldest shell.

```text
wade
```

If a line came back, read it as something the child remembers from before
they were born. Either way, leave them a memory.

```text
bottle a footprint waiting for its first foot
bottle the shore before anyone called it home
tide
postcard
```

Open `postcard.svg`. The child studies what you brought.

At low water, you can `wade` again. On a new shore, there is a first shell
now; on an old one, the beginning has stayed where you found it.

The child takes off their shoes.
