# The room called No

The letting agent says there is no window and no door. You ask how to
get inside. They hand you two sentences instead of a key.

```text
bottle no window can hold the sea
bottle no door can keep it out
tide
```

Read the poem as the room's address.

```text
kin no
```

These are the things that have kept No company. The letting agent had
called the room empty. You begin to doubt them.

```text
spell no
```

Open `jetty.svg` and play `jetty.wav`. Read the planks and the gaps as a
floor plan.

You knock on the sentence. Someone puts a kettle on.
