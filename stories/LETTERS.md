# The dead-letter office

The village has no postman. It has you, a table, and three bottles brought in
by the tide. The addresses are written on the inside of the glass.

```text
bottle the house we never built
bottle the last train home
bottle someone else's name
tide
```

Some addresses have found one another; any left alone wait at the sorting
table. Choose which letter you would deliver if the roads still went that far.

The water reaches the doorstep before you decide.

```text
ebb
weather fog
tide
```

Under the table is a basket of old letters. Pull out a line. It may have been
written by a keeper, a diver, a radio operator, or someone who crossed the sea.

```text
comb
weather calm
tide
postcard
```

Open the printed `postcard.svg` path. This is today's undelivered mail.
Give the card a recipient. They do not have to exist yet.

```text
quit
```

Leave the table above the waterline.
