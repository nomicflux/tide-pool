# The unfinished jetty

Seven planks lie on the harbour steps. Someone has marked their ends
H A R B O U R. The carpenter left before the tide returned.

```text
spell harbour
```

Open `jetty.svg` through Keepsakes or the printed path. A remembered line
stretches out from each letter the shore can answer; bare letters mark gaps.
Play `jetty.wav`: each plank chimes, each gap is a silent step.

```text
bottle a plank long enough for a name
drop hammer salt nail
tide
```

The new poem joins the shore. At this pause, type `spell` followed by your
name, then open the new `jetty.svg` and play `jetty.wav`. Read and hear
whatever holds. Continue with an empty Enter.

```text
stars
```

Open `sky.svg` through Keepsakes or the printed path. These are the words
that have stayed with the shore most often.

The carpenter has left you the hammer.
