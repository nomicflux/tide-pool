# A house someone said

A hermit crab waits beside a shell too small for it. On this coast, words
can be houses. Ask where it lives.

```text
crab
```

If it has an address, read the whole line as a floor plan. If it has none,
there is room to build.

```text
bottle a house with a window under the water
drop shell door home
tide
crab
```

Open `crab.svg` through Keepsakes or the printed path. The crab may have
moved, or kept the older room.

At this pause, you may add a `bottle` with a room of your own, make a `tide`,
then ask `crab` again. Continue with an empty Enter.

```text
postcard
```

Open `postcard.svg` through Keepsakes or the printed path.

Outside the house, two small tracks cross out the word vacant.
