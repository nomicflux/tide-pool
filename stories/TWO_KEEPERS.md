# Two keepers, one lamp

Alice keeps the lamp until Bob arrives. Bob keeps it until Alice returns.
Most nights, their names pass in opposite directions on the stairs.

```text
drop Alice Bob lamp letter
seek Alice Bob
```

If the names met, read their line as a handoff. If they missed each other,
the lamp still needs tending.

```text
signal the lamp is lit
```

A dot is a brief light; a dash stays lit three times as long. Tap one word
on the desk for the keeper who has not arrived.

Before the next shift, you may leave a message with `bottle` followed by a
phrase. Press Enter on an empty line when you are ready to hand over.

```text
tide
kin Alice
kin Bob
bind
```

Open the book through the browser's Keepsakes link or the printed path.
The keepers may remember different company.

```text
quit
```

Leave the lamp where the next hand can find it.
