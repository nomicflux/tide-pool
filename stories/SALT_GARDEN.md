# The salt garden

Nothing grows in this soil, says the gardener, except what you meant to say.
She gives you two seeds wrapped in a page torn from a tide table.

```text
bottle a seed kept warm inside a closed hand
bottle a seed carried home in the hem of a coat
tide
```

The gardener reads the rows with her finger.

```text
trace seed
```

If something else belongs here, `bottle` it, then make a `tide`.
The gardener leaves a space beside your words.

You ask when to come back. She puts the watering can outside to catch the rain.
