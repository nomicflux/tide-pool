# The tide-mark painter

On the harbour wall, a painter keeps two marks for every word: a low one for
the tide that first carried it in, and a high one for the tide that last did.
Tonight she has fresh paint and one word to mark.

```text
bottle a line of white paint along the harbour wall
drop salt
tide
first salt
last salt
wall salt
```

Open `wall.svg` to see salt’s marks.

If both marks name the same tide, salt is new here and you watched it arrive.
If they are far apart, the wall has held salt for many tides, and the paint
between the marks is older than you are.

Choose a word from the line the tide just kept, not from a shell it washed
back. Type `first` and then the word to find its low mark, then `last` and
the word to find its high one.

```text
ebb all
bottle the water remembers the wall
bottle the wall remembers the water
tide
last wall
wall wall
```

Open `wall.svg` again. The newest mark is still wet.
The painter leaves the brush in the bucket for
whoever brings the next word.

```text
quit
```
