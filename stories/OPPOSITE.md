# The opposite shore

Every night, a light appears where the chart shows open water.
Tonight it asks a question.

```text
signal are you there
```

Listen to `signal.wav`, or watch the lamp in `signal.svg`, through the
browser's Keepsakes links or the printed paths. The short notes and quick
flashes are dots; the long ones are dashes.

At this pause, type `signal` followed by your answer. Listen to the new
recording, or watch your own lamp, before you continue.

```text
bottle a light where there should be water
drop reply distance
tide
```

Read the poem as what arrived on the other shore.

```text

```

Play `signal.wav` again, or watch `signal.svg`. The lamp is sending the last
line back to you.
