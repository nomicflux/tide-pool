# The inquiry desk

The clerk has a drawer for every question and no paper for writing answers.
She listens through the open window.

```text
drop key window visitor
tide
ask who left the key
```

Read the answer as a clue. The sea can only offer something already said on
this shore.

At this pause, type `ask` followed by your own question. If the sea has no
answer yet, leave the question on the desk. Continue with an empty Enter.

```text
bottle the person on the other side
tide
ask who is at the window
```

Choose one word from the answer as the name on the envelope.

```text
bind
```

The book holds everything the clerk could have heard. Open it if you want
to question her choice.

She files the answer under yesterday. Your question is dated tomorrow.
