# The cloakroom

The attendant takes your coat without asking your name.
She asks whether you have emptied the pockets.

```text
bottle a coat that has learned the shape of waiting
bottle a pocket keeping something its owner forgot
```

If something is still in your pocket, `bottle` it here.
Otherwise, let the attendant check the lining.

```text
tide
```

The poem is your claim ticket. Read it before you leave.
The attendant holds out a coat. You try the pockets first.
