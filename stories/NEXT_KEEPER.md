# The next keeper

In the museum, a room holds the keepers' hundredth shift.

Carry the last line out. Its words are allowed to move again.

```text
visit hundredth-tide
bottle the hundred and first light
tide
```

Read the new poem as the note left for your shift. The room behind you
keeps its old ending.

```text
signal the lamp is still lit
```

Watch `signal.svg` or play `signal.wav` through Keepsakes or the printed
paths. At this pause, you can send your own signal and watch or hear it
before continuing.

```text
spell keepers
```

Open `jetty.svg` and play `jetty.wav`. A silent step is a letter still
waiting for its plank.

The next keeper does not have to know your name to find the light.
