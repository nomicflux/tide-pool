# The census of the wrack line

Once a year someone walks the high-water mark and counts what the sea has
kept. This year it is you. Begin with what the shore remembers most:

```text
strandline
```

If the beach is bare, you are the first to walk it. If it is not, those heaps
were left by everyone before you: a keeper, a diver, a navigator, a clerk of
dead letters. Pick one thing up and read where it came from.

```text
comb
```

The census needs a column for things nobody counts. Bottle one, and set two
ordinary finds beside it:

```text
bottle the hour before the tide turns
drop driftwood glass
weather calm
tide
```

Write the poem in the ledger. Then the wind changes.

```text
weather storm
ebb
tide
```

Something was never counted after all. Census-takers have a phrase for that:
lost at sea. Walk the line once more. Your own poems are in the heaps now.

```text
strandline
```

Close the ledger.

```text
quit
```

Next year someone else will walk this line, and count you.
