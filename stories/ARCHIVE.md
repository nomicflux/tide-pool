# The archive of open windows

Behind the dead-letter office, a clerk keeps every sentence the coast has
said. She is looking for the first person who mentioned a window.

```text
drop window salt thread
tide
first window
trace window
```

The clerk labels the first appearance.
On a new shore, it may begin with you.
On an older one, the window may already have belonged to several lives.

Choose another word from a line and type `trace` followed by that word.
Press Enter on an empty line when you are ready to return to the clerk.

```text
comb
tide
trace window
```

The window has entered another sentence while you were away. Decide which
line the clerk should pin above her desk. You may bottle that line before
continuing.

```text
postcard
```

The postcard is the collection on your desk. The archive keeps the rest.

```text
quit
```

The clerk leaves a window open for the next sentence.
