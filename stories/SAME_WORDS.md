# The same words

Two windows face the harbour. In the first, a keeper writes a sentence.
In the second, someone who has never met them writes it too.

```text
bottle I am still here
bottle I am still here
tide
```

Read the repeated words aloud. Give each voice a different reason for staying.

```text
cast I am still here
```

One copy leaves in a bottle, beyond your reach for this visit.

```text
find
```

If words arrived, they came from another bottle. Even if they match yours,
someone let go of them separately. If nothing arrived, leave the second
window lit.

```text
postcard
```

Open `postcard.svg` through Keepsakes or the printed path.

From the harbour, the windows look like one light reflected twice.
