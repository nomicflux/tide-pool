# The mapmaker

The mapmaker asks for the name of the place you are going.
You point at her chart. She turns the paper over.

```text
bottle maps drawn by someone who has never left
bottle maps folded by someone who cannot stay
tide
```

She lays out the letters the shore remembers.

```text
letters
```

For a traveller who has not chosen a destination, she draws this:

```text
spell MAZE
```

Open `jetty.svg`. Each plank is a remembered line. A bare letter is a place
the shore has not filled yet.

If another word would take you home, `spell` it here and open the drawing again.

She folds the paper. One corner will not quite meet the other.
