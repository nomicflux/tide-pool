In Tide Pool, type `read` followed by a place number, such as `read 1`.

**1 — [The lighthouse](stories/LIGHTHOUSE.md).** Keep one last shift for a ship that
never arrives.

**2 — [The bell](stories/BELLS.md).** Dive beneath the harbour with five gifts. Bring
back a sound.

**3 — [The radio station](stories/RADIO.md).** Tune a receiver by taking things away.
Hear what remains.

**4 — [The open water](stories/CROSSING.md).** Wait for the spring tide, then cross
with what the shore gives you.

**5 — [The dead-letter office](stories/LETTERS.md).** Sort bottled addresses and send
a postcard to someone who may not exist.

**6 — [The wrack line](stories/WRACK.md).** Walk the high-water mark and count
what every visitor left.

**7 — [The inn](stories/INN.md).** Check in with what you are carrying. Leave
with another name.

**8 — [The observatory](stories/OBSERVATORY.md).** Read the stars made by the
shore's remembered words.

**9 — [The archive](stories/ARCHIVE.md).** Follow a word through the coast's
earlier voices.

**10 — [The lamp room](stories/TWO_KEEPERS.md).** Wait for two keepers to meet,
then leave something for the next shift.

**11 — [The frozen harbour](stories/FROZEN.md).** Walk out on the ice with what
you could not put down, then wait for the thaw.

**12 — [The tide pool museum](stories/MUSEUM.md).** Walk through one room for each
thing the sea learned to do.

**13 — [The kitchen at dawn](stories/BREAKFAST.md).** Leave something for the
other keeper.

**14 — [The inquiry desk](stories/INQUIRY.md).** Find an answer in the shore's
remembered lines.

**15 — [The harbour steps](stories/BOTTLE.md).** Look for a bottle and leave
words for a later visitor.

**16 — [The unfinished jetty](stories/JETTY.md).** Lay a line where a letter
waits over water.

**17 — [The end of the jetty](stories/JETTY_END.md).** Read the echoes and
send a message back toward shore.

**18 — [The market](stories/MARKET.md).** Ask what morning looks like and
bring something for the stalls.

**19 — [The crab's house](stories/CRAB.md).** Find the shore's resident and
offer it a room.

**20 — [The harbour office](stories/HARBOURMASTER.md).** Count the coast; keep a chair
for someone expected.

**21 — [The waiting room](stories/WAITING_ROOM.md).** Buy a ticket
to yesterday.

**22 — [The weather laundromat](stories/WEATHER_WASH.md).** Wash a borrowed sky
through fog, storm, and calm.

**23 — [The lowest step](stories/FIRST_FOOTPRINT.md).** Reach the oldest shell
at low water; leave a memory for a child.

**24 — [The shoe repair shop](stories/SHOE_REPAIR.md).** Have a worn-out road
stitched back together.

**25 — [The newspaper stand](stories/NEWSPAPER.md).** Read tomorrow’s headline
and send it out to sea.

**26 — [The station clock](stories/DATELINE.md).** Turn the ledger of days
and add a poem under today's date.

**27 — [The name-return counter](stories/BORROWED_NAME.md).** Leave a borrowed
name before morning.

**28 — [The quiet room](stories/QUIET_ROOM.md).** Empty the pool; find the bell
that stayed in the shore.

**29 — [The seawall](stories/GULL.md).** Let a gull take one thing; make a poem
from what remains.

**30 — [The salt garden](stories/SALT_GARDEN.md).** Plant what you meant to say.

**31 — [The slipway](stories/SLIPWAY.md).** Read the timetable and wade if
the water allows.

**32 — [The ferry](stories/FERRY.md).** Pay with the sea’s last words.

**33 — [The photographer](stories/PHOTOGRAPHER.md).** Sit for a portrait of what is missing.

**34 — [The darkroom](stories/DARKROOM.md).** Develop a face and a pencilled name.

**35 — [The bindery](stories/BINDERY.md).** Find a line to fold a corner for.

**36 — [The mapmaker](stories/MAPMAKER.md).** Choose a word to travel by.

**37 — [The cloakroom](stories/CLOAKROOM.md).** Check your pockets.

**38 — [The tide-mark painter](stories/TIDEMARK.md).** Mark a word’s first and latest tides.

Every place shares one shore. A line left by the keeper can wash up in
the diver's hands, or speak through the station's aerial. Visit in any order.
