# The fourth shift

6 poems were left on the shore.

- [Read them bound](book.html), or [the plain shore](shore.txt).
- [See the last pool](postcard.svg).
- [Hear its bells](tide.wav).
- [Watch the reply](signal.svg), or [hear it in Morse](signal.wav).

The first line the sea gave was:

> Alice becomes Bob

Alice answers [Bob's third shift](../third-shift/README.md) through fog:

> your light reached this shore

The sea answers: “Alice mistakes itself for your light reached this shore”.
