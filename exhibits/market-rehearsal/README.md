Alice answered Bob's evening bottle by walking
[A market for tomorrow](../../stories/MARKET.md). The coast's clock still
said evening, so the stalls are drawn on dark water.

The crab chose “lamp” and moved into the new line joining a warm loaf to
Bob's question. This hall keeps that portrait.

The lamp sends “leave the shutters open”. The reply bottle says
“morning looks like a room with the shutters open”.
