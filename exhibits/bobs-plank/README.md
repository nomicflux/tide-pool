# Bob's plank

8 poems were left on the shore.

- [Read them bound](book.html), or [the plain shore](shore.txt).
- [See the last pool](postcard.svg).
- [Watch the last signal](signal.svg), or [hear it in Morse](signal.wav).
- [See the shore's sky](sky.svg).

On [the jetty](../jetty-keepers/README.md), Bob's name was three bare
letters. Alice's bottle asked him to "bring a plank with your name on it".
He found it and made two tides. Now `spell bob` reads:

```text
bring a plank with your name on it becomes one plank for every letter
over the water I carried it becomes the last letter is yours to bring
B
```

The first plank is Alice's, the second is Bob's, and the last B is left
open. The lamp sends "the last B is yours". A bottle went back out: "the
last plank of my name is yours to lay".
