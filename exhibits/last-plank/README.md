Bob left [the last letter](../bobs-plank/README.md) for Alice. With her
new line, `spell Bob` reads:

```text
bring a plank with your name on it becomes one plank for every letter
over the water I carried it becomes the last letter is yours to bring
beside your name I left room for the next keeper waits beneath Bob, here is the last plank
```

The lamp sends “your name reaches the water”.
