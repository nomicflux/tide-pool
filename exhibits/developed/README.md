The sea's last words were:

> a face the paper almost remembers remembers a name written on the back in pencil

Bob developed [the darkroom](../../stories/DARKROOM.md) print (stop 34): in
fog it came up as

> a name written on the back in pencil haunts a face the paper almost remembers

and in calm water it cleared to the line above. The pool was rebuilt from
the story's two bottles, in calm, before hanging.
