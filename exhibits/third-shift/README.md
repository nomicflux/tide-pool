# The third shift

5 poems were left on the shore.

- [Read them bound](book.html), or [the plain shore](shore.txt).
- [See the last pool](postcard.svg).
- [Hear its bells](tide.wav).

The first line the sea gave was:

> Alice becomes Bob

The third shift on the [Two Keepers shore](../two-keepers/README.md), after
[the next shift](../next-shift/README.md). Bob echoed the sea's last words,
"Bob holds and", and bottled a reply:

> I found it lit and left it burning

Bob met Alice after ten tides, as she had met him: "Alice shelters Bob".
