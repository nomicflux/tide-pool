# An invitation to the harbour

4 poems were left on the shore.

- [Read them bound](book.html), or [the plain shore](shore.txt).
- [See the last pool](postcard.svg).
- [Hear its bells](tide.wav).

Alice found the reply from [the answering window](../answering-window/README.md).
The tide joined it to a new thought:

> so am I, in the window across the water remembers the tide has learned our addresses

The lamp sends “meet me at fifteen”. A bottle carries the invitation to
the harbour steps, place 15 on [the chart](../../CHART.md).
