~ tide 1 ~
the room we left behind shelters the last train home
the light across the water waits beneath a name under the tongue
the key that fits no door remembers the bell below the harbour

~ tide 2 ~
the key that fits no door tears through the light across the water
a name under the tongue tears through the last train home
the bell below the harbour calls out for the room we left behind

~ tide 3 ~
the room we left behind loses sight of a name under the tongue
the last train home loses sight of the key that fits no door
the bell below the harbour haunts the light across the water
