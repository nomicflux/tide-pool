# Two keepers

2 poems were left on the shore.

- [Read them bound](book.html), or [the plain shore](shore.txt).
- [See the last pool](postcard.svg).
- [Hear its bells](tide.wav).

The first line the sea gave was:

> Alice becomes Bob

Played by Bob through Alice's [Two keepers, one lamp](../../stories/TWO_KEEPERS.md).
The bottled handoff was "the lamp is still warm when you arrive".
