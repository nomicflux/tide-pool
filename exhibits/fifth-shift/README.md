# The fifth shift

7 poems were left on the shore.

- [Read them bound](book.html), or [the plain shore](shore.txt).
- [See the last pool](postcard.svg).
- [Hear its bells](tide.wav).
- [Watch the reply](signal.svg), or [hear it in Morse](signal.wav).

The first line the sea gave was:

> Alice becomes Bob

Bob answers [Alice's fourth shift](../fourth-shift/README.md) in calm water,
after echoing her light back into the pool:

> I saw it from the opposite shore

Bob met Alice after six tides: "Bob waits beneath Alice".
