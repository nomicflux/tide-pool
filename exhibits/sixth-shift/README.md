# The sixth shift

9 poems were left on the shore.

- [Read them bound](book.html), or [the plain shore](shore.txt).
- [See the last pool](postcard.svg).
- [Hear its bells](tide.wav).
- [Watch the last signal](signal.svg), or [hear it in Morse](signal.wav).

The first line the sea gave was:

> Alice becomes Bob

After [the fifth shift](../fifth-shift/README.md) and a long night away,
Alice leaves bread for Bob.

> the key between us waits beneath bread for Bob

The lamp says “good morning”.
