# After midnight

Four bottled memories spent a night in fog, calm, and storm. Lines returned
from the shore until the guests dreamed in one another's words. Three poems remain.

- [Read them bound](book.html), or [the plain shore](shore.txt).
- [See the last pool](postcard.svg).
- [Hear its bells](tide.wav).

The first line the sea gave was:

> the staircase between dreams haunts the guest who arrived tomorrow
