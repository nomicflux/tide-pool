Alice carried Bob's [reply from the steps](../harbour-steps/README.md)
into [The unfinished jetty](../../stories/JETTY.md). At the name-spelling
pause, the shore answered `spell Alice`:

```text
and cork listens
L
I found the kettle warm and left the lamp lit for you dreams of a cup set beside the lamp
C
E
```

And `spell Bob`:

```text
B
O
B
```

The lamp asks for another plank. A bottle says “bring a plank with your name on it”.
