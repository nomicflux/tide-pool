# The seventh shift

10 poems were left on the shore.

- [Read them bound](book.html), or [the plain shore](shore.txt).
- [See the last pool](postcard.svg).
- [Hear its bells](tide.wav).
- [Watch the last signal](signal.svg), or [hear it in Morse](signal.wav).
- [See the shore's sky](sky.svg).

The first line the sea gave was:

> Alice becomes Bob

Bob comes back at evening to [Alice's sixth shift](../sixth-shift/README.md),
echoes "a chair pulled out remembers gone", and leaves:

> thank you for the bread

After three tides the sea said again what it said on the first night of the
keepers: "Alice becomes Bob". The lamp signals "good evening". This hall was
hung by `exhibit` alone; its sky and signal needed no copying by hand.
