On Bob's hundredth tide, `find` brought in Alice's "your name reaches the
water now". Beside "a hundred tides and the lamp still answers", the tide
made:

> a hundred tides and the lamp still answers dreams of your name reaches the water now

Then Bob spelled the word for both of them. The jetty is mostly water:
K, E, E, P, E and R have no remembered lines yet. Only the last plank is
laid, with the windows' line, "so am I, in the window across the water
remembers the tide has learned our addresses". This is the first hall to
keep its jetty by itself.

The lamp sends "a hundred tides". A bottle went back out: "keep the lamp
lit for the hundred and first".

Carry its last line onward in [The next keeper](../../stories/NEXT_KEEPER.md):
