The sea's last words were:

> a bell heard through a wall becomes a voice mistaken for the rain

Bob walked [the quiet room](../../stories/QUIET_ROOM.md) and left tide 50.
After the words ebbed away, `trace bell` found the line in the shore.
Alice kept the room empty: no words on the postcard, no bells.
The dial records rising water when this hall was hung.
