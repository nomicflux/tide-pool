Bob answered [the invitation](../harbour-invitation/README.md) by walking
[No address on the glass](../../stories/BOTTLE.md), place 15 on the chart.
The horizon showed one bottle; `find` brought in Alice's "meet me at the
harbour steps". With the story's cork and doorstep, the tide made:

> doorstep shelters meet me at the harbour steps
> and cork listens

The lamp sends "I came". A bottle went back out: "I came to the steps and
left the cork in the doorstep".
