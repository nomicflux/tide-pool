Alice walked [The end of the jetty](../../stories/JETTY_END.md) and found
Bob's invitation. Her answer became:

> meet me at the end of the jetty remembers I waited until the water stopped looking like distance

The lamp sends “here”. The bottle going back toward shore says
“the end of the jetty is another beginning”.
