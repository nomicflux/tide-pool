Bob arrived after [two windows](../two-windows/README.md). The greeting said
a bottle drifted offshore; `find` brought in Alice's "I am still here". He
answered beside it, and the tide made:

> I am still here remembers so am I, in the window across the water

The lamp sends "so am I". The answer went back out to sea in a bottle, for
whoever lights the next window.
