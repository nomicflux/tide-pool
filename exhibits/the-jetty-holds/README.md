At [the hundredth tide](../hundredth-tide/README.md), "keepers" was six
letters over water. Over four turns the keepers laid a plank each time a
bottle came in: Alice the K and two Es, Bob an E and the P. Bob's first
attempt at R tided into an S. Alice sent that rope back out to sea, and
this time it led the tide. `spell keepers` now reads:

```text
keep the lamp lit for the hundred and first dreams of keepers carry the light one plank farther
every light needs someone to return shelters and so I came back with a lantern
each keeper who reads this owes the jetty an E dreams of every returning lantern leaves a mark
pilings driven where the windows shine becomes one for each keeper
even the empty windows face the water becomes each remembered name needs somewhere to stand
rope from the last post to the lamp remembers and both keepers walk out together
so am I, in the window across the water remembers the tide has learned our addresses
```

No planks are missing. The lamp sends "the jetty holds". A bottle went
out: "meet me at the end of the jetty".
